# Travel

Here are the approximate costs to attend a Timber Tour stop or stops.

Athletes that are not in the competitive program will also incur
coaching costs (shared between athletes attending). Costs shown
include travel, food, coaching, and accommodations.

Travel grants to cover ferry travel are available through the
[BC Ferries Sport Experience Program](https://viasport.ca/grants/bc-ferries-sport-experience-program/).
Athletes 18 and under, or a parent or coach applying for them, can
apply. Apply at least 10 business days before travel. More detail is
on [Safety & Support](/support/#ferries). Athletes and their families
are responsible for organizing this travel voucher. For more
information, contact [](email:travel)
