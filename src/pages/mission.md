# The Club

Mount Washington Freestyle Club is a member-operated local club that offers
freestyle skiing programs, aimed at the expanding freestyle scene, for
both the recreational and competitive skier, from beginner to aspiring
international competitor. Coaches certified under the National Coaching
Certification Program (NCCP) offer children and teenagers the skills to
ski and compete safely. Most of our skiers would agree that meeting new
friends and having lots of fun have always been high on the agenda!


## Our Mission

Our primary goal is to introduce freestyle skiing in a safe and friendly environment on Vancouver Island. As well as developing youth athletes' abilities, the club provides a nurturing grassroots level for young skiers and their families to build their skills and confidence.

Our skiers develop strength, agility, balance as well as overall health,
fitness, confidence, and drive. The Club continues to work with national
and provincial freestyle skiing organizations to develop
high-performance athletes. We firmly believe that grassroots development
is what drives the discovery of future Olympians.
