# Volunteer

Mount Washington Freestyle Club needs all the helping hands that we
can get throughout the entire year but especially in the winter. To
keep our club running, we require that each family volunteers a
minimum of four shifts per child during the season. The volunteer
deposit has been added to program fees.

Use TeamSnap each week to sign up for your volunteer shifts.

Examples of some volunteer tasks:

- Saturdays & Sundays -- Lunch Supervision Duties
- Saturdays & Sundays -- Hill Prep
- Photographer

Thank you.
