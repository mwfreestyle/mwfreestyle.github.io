A Competitive program for athletes who love big mountain skiing
and has reached the appropriate skill level. Coaching included
for 2 IFSA events. Freeride is for skiers who are choosing
to make a commitment to developing and refining their
big mountain skills and taking their freestyle tricks to natural
terrain. Skiers are placed in groups based on ability and age at
the coach's discretion.

Athletes will continue to be exposed to all aspects of freestyle
skiing while specializing in freeride big mountain skiing.

Athletes are expected to show their stuff in
competition. [IFSA](https://ifsafreeride.org/) has a series of competitions (off
Island) athletes may be interested in. If interested, please
contact our Program Director, Peter Stubbs at
[](email:headCoach).
