Air sense is an essential component of freestyle skiing.
Trampoline training is offered to improve the agility,
coordination, strength and endurance necessary to safely advance
Freestyle Skiing skills. At Airhouse, Certified Instructors lead
games and challenges including obstacle courses and parkour
skills that build balance and coordination skills.

Trampoline is the perfect pre-season launchpad to shake
off the rust. Registrants must be currently registered in an MWFC
program. Skiers are divided into groups based on ability and age
at the Head Coach's discretion.

> We encourage our athletes to participate in trampoline to
> improve their air sense.
