An intermediate program for athletes considering competing in
the sport, or athletes who have reached the appropriate skill
level. Jr Freestylerz is for skiers age 9 to 12 who are choosing
to make a commitment to developing and refining their
slopestyle, moguls, and big air skills. Skiers are placed in
groups based on ability and age at the coach's discretion.

Athletes will continue to be exposed to all aspects of freestyle
skiing along with all the powder and steeps Mount Washington has to
offer.

Athletes are encouraged to show their stuff in
competition. Mount Washington Resort has some competitions during
the season. Also, Freestyle BC has a series of competitions (off
Island) athletes may be interested in. If interested, please
contact our Program Director, Peter Stubbs at
[](email:headCoach).
