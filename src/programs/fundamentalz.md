---
program: fundamentalz
---

Skiers must be able to ski parallel on Blue runs, be able to load and unload the Eagle and Whiskey jack chair unassisted and take care of their own equipment. Athletes in the Fundamentalz level will be taught mountain safety, all terrain skiing, jumps and bumps skills and will be introduced to the Terrain Park. This is a full day program running from 9AM to 3PM, skiers must have stamina to last the full day. If the skier is found to not meet the minimum ability on the first day a full refund of MWFC program fees will be provided.

Skiers are divided into groups based on ability and age at coach’s discretion. Athletes that are moved (at the Head Coach’s discretion) to the Jr. Freestylerz program will need to pay the additional fee.
